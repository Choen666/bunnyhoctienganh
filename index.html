About

A researcher's case for law, told in her own words

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of International Law at the Diplomatic Academy of Vietnam, where I am building a foundation in legal research, corporate compliance, and the regulatory frameworks that govern cross-border trade. Law, to me, is less a set of rules than a language — one that lets very different economies, cultures, and institutions negotiate a shared future.

My work sits at the intersection of three habits I keep returning to: reading primary sources closely, translating meaning across languages, and asking how a rule actually behaves once it leaves the page. That combination has taken me from academic conferences to business meeting rooms, and from legal drafting to live interpretation — always in service of the same question: how do people and institutions cooperate across a border, fairly and well?
